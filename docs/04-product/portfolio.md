## Product principles

1. Every product exists to prove or scale the visibility promise ("impossible to ignore online").
2. Services deliverables should become templates (`templates/`, when created) so the next client costs less to serve.
3. Product docs state what IS and what is TARGET, never blending the two.

## What we do not build

- Custom software outside the visibility/marketing/web domain.
- Anything requiring us to store card data or move money ourselves (Flutterwave owns that).
- Unpaid speculative client work.
