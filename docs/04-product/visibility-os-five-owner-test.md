# Visibility OS: five-owner test (XP-06, UB-01, XP-08)

Goal: find out, with real people and no help from us, whether the first result makes sense. Plan ~15 minutes each. Run on the person's own phone. Written 2026-10-12.

## Who (five people)

| # | Who | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Business owner with a website | Group 1 |
| 2 | Business owner with a website (different line of work) | Group 1 |
| 3 | Salon, caterer or shop that sells on Instagram or WhatsApp, no website | Group 2 |
| 4 | Another Instagram or WhatsApp seller | Group 2 |
| 5 | A creator, freelancer or coach | Group 3 |

Not friends who will be polite. People who would actually buy or ignore this. Do not explain the product first.

## What you say (the only thing)

"Here is a free tool. Use it on your own business as if I was not here. Say out loud what you are thinking. I cannot help, but I will write things down."

Hand them the link: https://tossvisibilityos.com

## What you watch (do not help, do not hint)

1. Which of the three choices do they pick, and is it the right one for them? (note: right / wrong / hesitated)
2. Do they finish the scan without asking anything? How long did it take from opening the page to seeing a result? (stopwatch)
3. At the result, say: "Without scrolling, what would you do first?" Start the clock. Write their answer word for word.
   - Pass: they name the step the page shows as first, in about 30 seconds, unaided.
4. "What does the score mean?" and "Is anything on this page wrong or unfair about you?" (a wrong or unfair line is the most valuable thing you can find)
5. "Would you send this to someone, or to a WhatsApp group?" Why or why not?
6. "Is there anything here that made you nervous, or you did not trust?"
7. Last: "What would you pay for, if anything?" (do not name a price)

## Record (one row per person)

| # | Right choice? | Finished unaided? | Seconds to result | First step named (30 s)? Pass? | Anything wrong or unfair | Would share? | Nervous about | Quote worth keeping |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Decide

- **Pass:** 4 of 5 name the right first step within 30 seconds, unaided, and at least 4 of 5 finish without help.
- **Any "wrong or unfair" line:** fix it before launch, even if everyone passes.
- **Two or more people stumble on the same thing:** that is the next change, not a one-off.
- Send me the table (a photo of your notes is enough) and I turn each problem into a test and a fix.

## Rules

- Do not ask them to sign up. Do not take payment. Do not show the founder's own scan.
- Tell each person their result page is private and can be deleted: send us the link and we delete it.
- Do not record their business details outside this sheet.
